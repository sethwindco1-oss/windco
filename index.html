How we clean

Four passes on every pane.

Scrub

A soaked strip washer loosens dirt, pollen and water spots from the glass.

Squeegee

One continuous pass pulls the water off so nothing dries into streaks.

Detail

Edges and corners get a lint-free towel to catch the last drips.

Finish

Sills and frames are wiped and screens go back in place.

Privacy policy

        
How we handle your information.

      

      

        
Effective November 11, 2026

        
WindCO Window Cleaning (“WindCO,” “we,” “us”) is a family-owned window cleaning business in Colorado. This policy explains what information we collect through windco.co and when you contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.


        
Information we collect

        
    
          
  • What you tell us. When you fill out a quote or booking form, we collect your name, phone number, email, address or neighborhood, details about your property, the services you want, your preferred date and any notes. If you use the holiday gift offer, we also collect the name and phone number of the friend or family member you list.
    • 
          
  • When you call, text or email us. We keep your phone number or email address and what you tell us, so we can follow up.
    • 
          
  • What we don’t collect. This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or ad tracking. Like any website, our hosting provider and the service that loads our fonts may receive basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address and browser type.
    • 
        


        
How we use it

        
    
          
  • To answer your questions and give you a free quote.
    • 
          
  • To schedule and do the work, and to contact you about your appointment.
    • 
          
  • To apply holiday deals and set up a gift discount for the person you name.
    • 
          
  • To keep the records any business needs, like invoices and tax records.
    • 
        

        
We don’t sell or rent your personal information to anyone.


        
Who we share it with

        
    
          
  • Our crew, so they know where to go and what to clean.
    • 
          
  • Companies that help us run the website and our email: Web3Forms delivers form requests to our inbox, Google provides our email and the website’s fonts, and Cloudflare hosts the website. They handle your information only to provide those services.
    • 
          
  • When the law requires it, for example to respond to a valid legal request.
    • 
        


        
How long we keep it

        
We keep your information while we’re providing services to you and for as long as we need it for our business records. After that, we delete it.


        
Your choices

        
You can ask us at any time to see the information we have about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can ask us to stop contacting you. Just call, text or email us using the details below.
