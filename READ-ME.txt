WINDCO WEBSITE - WHAT EACH FILE DOES
====================================

index.html ............ The page skeleton: all the words and sections for every tab
                        (Holiday landing page, Family Gift, Home, About, Services,
                        Plans, Free Quote, Privacy, Thank-you page).

css/styles.css ........ All the colors, fonts, sizes and layout (how it looks).

js/1-window-game.js ............ Home page squeegee game (scrub the whole window, then click Squeegee).
js/2-page-tabs.js .............. Switches between tabs/pages (the #holiday, #home, #quote... links).
js/3-holiday-checklist-window.js The Christmas checklist window game on the landing page.
js/4-try-it-hand.js ............ The little "try it" hand hint that shows people how to play.
js/5-form-tools.js ............. Shared form helpers: Web3Forms keys, phone dashes, email check,
                                 calendar, time dropdown, Google Maps link, thank-you page.
js/6-holiday-booking-form.js ... The holiday booking form on the landing page.
js/7-free-quote-form.js ........ The free quote form on the main site.

images/favicon.png ............. The little icon in the browser tab.
images/windco-logo-snow.png .... WindCO logo with snow (main site header).
images/windco-logo-gold-snow.png Gold snowy logo (holiday landing page).

og-image.png .......... Preview picture when someone shares windco.co in a text/Facebook.
robots.txt ............ Tells Google it may read the site.
sitemap.xml ........... List of pages for Google.
